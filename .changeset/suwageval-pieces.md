New pieces for moving suwageval off Mantine, none breaking:

- `Tooltip`: a short explanation beside any element on hover or focus; `disabled` leaves the element alone.
- `Spinner`: a status named by its `label`, in the sizes `md`, `sm` and `xs`.
- `SegmentedControl`: one of a few options, all visible, as a radio group.
- `CopyButton`: copies its `value` and reads `Copied` until the value changes.
- `ConfirmDialog` takes `confirmDisabled` (for example until a name is typed); `Dialog` and `ConfirmDialog` take `width="wide"`.
